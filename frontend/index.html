What BhoomiShield Does

BhoomiShield solves the systemic challenges in traditional land records management—such as fraudulent transfers, boundary overlaps, multiple deed registrations, and slow mutation lifecycles. By integrating GIS cadastral boundaries with spatial coordinates, BhoomiShield provides a single source of truth for landholders, buyers, financial institutions, and government revenue departments.

Each land parcel is assigned a unique 14-digit Bhu-Aadhaar (ULPIN) number, binding the legal khatian, register-II details, plot coordinates, and mutation history into an immutable verification ledger.

Core Capabilities & Digital Services

1. 14-Digit ULPIN (Bhu-Aadhaar)

Geo-referenced unique identifier based on international ECEF/WGS-84 vertex coordinates, eliminating duplicate registrations and parcel identity disputes.

2. Interactive Cadastral GIS Maps

High-precision cadastral polygon mapping with multi-layer overlays for Mauza, Tehsil, Khatian, Plot borders, and satellite imagery verification.

3. Tamper-Proof Audit Trail

Cryptographic hashing and timestamped mutation ledger ensuring every ownership change, correction, or sub-division is permanently verifiable.

4. Automated e-Mutation Workflow

Streamlined digital application, objection filing, and verification by Circle Officers (CO) and Revenue Sub-Inspectors (CI) with real-time status alerts.

5. Dispute & Encroachment Detection

AI and spatial algorithms analyzing plot overlap, stay orders, litigations, and buffer-zone violations to score transaction risk before property purchases.

6. Secure Citizen OTP Authentication

Multi-factor authentication via automated email OTP dispatch, secure password resets, and role-based permissions protecting citizen land portfolios.

How the Land Verification Workflow Operates

  1. 1

    Parcel Search & Spatial Inspection

    Enter State, District, Anchal/Circle, Mauza, and Plot Number to pull up certified land records and boundary coordinates.

  2. 2

    Title & Encumbrance Verification

    Cross-verify land type, recorded tenant (Raiyat), area in acres/decimals, and check for active court injunctions or loan mortgages.

  3. 3

    Digital e-Mutation & Notice Period

    Submit transfer deed documents online. Automated 14-day statutory public notice issued with instant objection logging.

  4. 4

    Immutable Bhu-Aadhaar Certificate Issuance

    Upon Circle Officer approval, Register-II is updated and a digitally signed Bhu-Aadhaar Land Identity Certificate is generated.

Trust, Security & Data Governance

BhoomiShield adheres to rigorous digital governance benchmarks established by the Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme (DILRMP). All sensitive API traffic is protected by end-to-end TLS/HTTPS encryption, strict Content Security Policies (CSP), HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), and role-based multi-factor access protocols.

Public records are synchronized with state revenue datasets from Jharbhoomi and state cadastre repositories, maintaining non-repudiation and transparency.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I find my plot's 14-digit ULPIN?

You can search by your District (e.g., Bokaro), Anchal (e.g., Chas), and Plot Number in the Land Records Search or directly locate your parcel on the Interactive Cadastral Map.

What should I do if my password needs to be reset?

Visit the Password Reset Portal, enter your registered email address, and you will receive a secure 6-digit OTP along with a one-click reset link.

Who can register on the BhoomiShield portal?

Both individual citizens (landowners, buyers) and authorized Revenue Department officials can register via the Citizen Registration gateway with instant email verification.
